## Platform

## Users

Primary: business buyers. That means companies wanting products under their own label, and hospitals or institutions buying supply. Shoppers and patients are secondary.

## Product Purpose

DIVISIRI offers medical nutrition and natural wellness products. On the website, business buyers should be able to review capability before they make contact.

## Positioning

One partner, three routes: DIVISIRI's own brands, private label, and institutional supply.

## Operating Context

- Private-label stages shown on the site: idea, formulation, development, quality, production, delivery.
- Availability, minimum quantities and timelines are confirmed project by project.
- Sales and partnership enquiries go to sales@divisiri.com. General and support enquiries go to admin@divisiri.com.
- The online shop is a separate project. The static site only links to it, and the links are hidden until the shop is live.
