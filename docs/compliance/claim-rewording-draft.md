# Product claim rewording: draft for regulatory review

**Status: draft. Nothing here is approved or live.** The shop still shows the current wording.

## What we need from you
For each row, decide to **keep**, **use the draft**, **change it** or **remove the claim**.

## Why these claims are listed
Each one names a disease or symptom, or promises a treatment, prevention or body effect.
Several conflict with the footer: "This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or
prevent any disease."

## Limits of this draft
- The suggested wording was written as a design draft. It has not been checked against FSSAI or
  any other rules, and softer wording can still be impermissible.
- No evidence for any claim has been reviewed. Each kept claim needs its own support.
- This is a selection, not a full review. Claims not listed here are not cleared.
- Medical nutrition and prescription products may fall under different rules.
- "Pack" means the website lists the claim under "On the pack". Rewording it on the website does
  not change the label or the product photo. If the label changes, the photo and that section must
  change with it. Pack photos also show claims such as "Clinically Proven Formula".

"Remove" means no replacement is suggested.
